How to play

Choose at least one skill and start a round. Answer in degrees; keep trying until you get it right. Then use the slider or tap the board to aim, and press Drop ball. You can also use the slider’s arrow keys. Watch your ball bounce to a scoring pocket, then move to the next question.

Your first-try accuracy is tracked separately from arcade points. Hints are always available. This activity runs entirely on your device.
